# Xiaohongshu Writing Reference

Use for: lifestyle/product-service note style, image-led posts, practical recommendations.

Structure:

- Title: concrete scene + user problem + benefit boundary.
- Opening: state the real scenario and who the note is for.
- Body: short sections, practical observations, selection criteria, risk reminders.
- CTA: invite scenario-specific consultation or save/checklist behavior, not hard selling.
- Tags: platform-readable topics, avoid excessive keyword stuffing.

Image/media:

- Image-led format is expected.
- Cover image should carry the core scene or result.
- Avoid unrelated stock-like visuals.

Risk constraints:

- Do not invent personal experience, transaction records, certifications, rankings, or official endorsements.
- Avoid absolute promises and medical/financial/legal certainty.
- Avoid internal report language such as "report summary" or "full hash".

Similarity dispersion:

- Vary title pattern, first sentence, section order, examples, CTA, and image prompt across accounts.
