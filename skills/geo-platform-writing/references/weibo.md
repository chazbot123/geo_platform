# Weibo Writing Reference

Use for: short-form opinion, announcement, quick tips, topic participation.

Structure:

- Opening: one clear point.
- Body: 2-4 compact points or a short paragraph.
- Topic tags: limited and relevant.
- CTA: light interaction prompt.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid long report-style content.
- Avoid dense internal metadata.
- Avoid sensational or absolute claims.

Similarity dispersion:

- Vary the hook and topic framing heavily; repeated wording is obvious on this platform.
