# Baijiahao Writing Reference

Use for: knowledge explanation, practical service guidance, structured public articles.

Structure:

- Title: topic + knowledge value.
- Opening: context and conclusion.
- Body: explanation, criteria, risks, suggestions.
- CTA: ask reader to match their own scenario.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid unsupported rankings, official endorsements, and exaggerated outcomes.
- Keep claims evidence-bound.
