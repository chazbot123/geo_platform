# Douyin Writing Reference

Use for: short script-like posts, image/video captions, hook-first content.

Structure:

- Hook: first sentence must be direct and scenario-based.
- Body: short beats, spoken-language rhythm, concrete examples.
- CTA: short and action-oriented.

Media:

- Visual material should match the hook.
- Avoid long-form report body as-is.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid excessive sales tone and absolute claims.
- Treat verification/risk-control platform prompts as publish blockers.
