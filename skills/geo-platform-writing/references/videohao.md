# Video Account Writing Reference

Use for: WeChat ecosystem video/image-text posts and short explanatory captions.

Structure:

- Opening: clear scenario or question.
- Body: short explanatory paragraphs or script beats.
- CTA: WeChat ecosystem-friendly, restrained, service-oriented.

Media:

- Video or image material must match the article claim.
- If media is missing, mark as publish blocker where automation requires it.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid unverifiable claims.
- Avoid platform-blocking sensitive content.
