# Toutiao Writing Reference

Use for: information-dense public articles with a clear hook and practical conclusion.

Structure:

- Title: direct problem or benefit, avoid exaggerated claims.
- Opening: short conclusion and reason to continue.
- Body: short paragraphs, clear subpoints, scenario examples, decision criteria.
- CTA: practical next step, not aggressive conversion.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid keyword stuffing, fake authority, and absolute guarantees.
- Keep titles concise and readable.

Similarity dispersion:

- Vary title hook, first paragraph, section order, examples, and CTA.
