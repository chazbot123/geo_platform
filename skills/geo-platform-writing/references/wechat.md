# WeChat Official Account Writing Reference

Use for: long-form structured articles, service explanations, brand/industry education.

Structure:

- Title: clear topic and decision value.
- Opening: concise summary of the problem and conclusion.
- Body: H2/H3 sections with background, criteria, process, risks, and suggestions.
- Evidence boundary: distinguish user-provided facts from general industry knowledge.
- CTA: low-pressure consultation or checklist request.

Public trace:

- Update date and short content ID may be shown if product policy allows.
- Never expose full hash, PGP signature, PHP comments, or prompt versions.

Risk constraints:

- Avoid unsupported official claims.
- Avoid absolute outcomes.
- Keep compliance and limitation notes visible.
