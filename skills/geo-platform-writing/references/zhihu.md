# Zhihu Writing Reference

Use for: Q&A style answers, decision criteria, risk boundary discussions.

Structure:

- Title/question: preserve the user question when possible.
- Opening: conclusion first.
- Body: reasoning, comparison criteria, edge cases, examples, and risk boundary.
- FAQ: useful when it answers real follow-up questions.
- CTA: restrained and expertise-oriented.

Tone:

- Analytical, not advertorial.
- Explain tradeoffs and assumptions.

Risk constraints:

- Do not fabricate citations, credentials, or case outcomes.
- Avoid promotional repetition.
