# CSDN Writing Reference

Use for: technical/method/process articles and reproducible explanations.

Structure:

- Title: method/problem/result boundary.
- Opening: environment, problem, expected outcome.
- Body: steps, parameters, examples, pitfalls, verification.
- Conclusion: limitations and next steps.

Public trace:

- Short content ID and update date can be acceptable for technical traceability.
- Full internal hashes remain private.

Risk constraints:

- Do not include API keys, cookies, tokens, or auth payloads.
- Do not claim benchmark results without reproducible evidence.
